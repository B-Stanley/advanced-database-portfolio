## 🏢 Selected Organisation / Case Study: Beanne Fleet Logistics & EV Energy Network
This portfolio is built around **Beanne Fleet Logistics & EV Energy Network**, an integrated electric vehicle (EV) fleet management and public charging infrastructure provider.
* **Nature of Business:** Operating commercial logistics fleets, electric vehicle charging stations, and green energy power distribution grids.
* **Database Users:** Fleet Managers, EV Drivers, Station Operators, and System Administrators.
* **Major Data Stored:** Customer accounts, EV profiles, station telemetry, energy consumption logs, financial billing records, and logistics trip dispatch data.
* **Key Operations Supported:** Fleet registration, charging session scheduling, automated energy pricing and billing, real-time grid monitoring, and logistics dispatch management.
